A little background

Learning, across disciplines and places.

My background spans physics in India, advanced technologies in Mexico, and quantum information in New York. Alongside research, I have taught more than 25 sections of college physics and astronomy to more than 1,000 students.

My teaching work includes creating free course materials and using demonstrations and simulations to help students connect the mathematics to physical ideas.

Research

I study questions in quantum information using theoretical models and numerical simulation.

Research areas

State learning

How can measurements reveal an unknown quantum state? We study what becomes possible when copies arrive in pairs.

Quantum algorithms for optimization

We investigate how quantum interference can help us search for useful solutions to optimization problems.

Sensor networks

We study how arrays of quantum sensors can recognize patterns in their environment.

Research

Quantum information through theory and computation

I study how quantum systems store, transform, and reveal information. My research uses theoretical models and numerical simulations to investigate learning, sensing, and quantum algorithms.

Research areas

Quantum-state learning

Learning properties of quantum states from paired, unlabeled quantum data.

Quantum sensing

Using arrays of qubits and entanglement to detect structure in incoming particle patterns.

Quantum algorithms

Studying interference-based methods for combinatorial optimization problems such as MaxCut.
